VulnLab Privacy Policy
======================
Last updated: Academic Year 2026-2027

1. Introduction
Welcome to VulnLab. We are committed to protecting the privacy of educational sandbox users.

2. Information Collection
This sandbox runs entirely locally on your machine and stores test data in a local SQLite database. No external telemetric data is collected or transmitted.

3. Educational Purpose
All mock data, credentials, and records provided herein are exclusively for security analysis, demonstration of OWASP vulnerabilities, and academic research.
